Project Overview

Designing Trust & Convenience

The Challenge

Pet owners struggle to find reliable, trustworthy dog walkers, while professionals lack a streamlined platform to showcase their services and manage bookings. The challenge was to create an intuitive mobile experience that builds trust and simplifies the entire process.

Key Features

  • ✓ Smart booking system with calendar integration
  • ✓ Verified walker profiles with ratings & reviews
  • ✓ Real-time GPS tracking during walks
  • ✓ Secure in-app payments
  • ✓ Photo & activity report sharing
  • ✓ Instant messaging between owners & walkers

Screen Designs

Onboarding & Login

Welcoming first-time users with clear value propositions and seamless social authentication options.

Home Dashboard

Personalized view showing upcoming walks, quick actions, and walker recommendations.

Walker Discovery

Browse and filter verified dog walkers with detailed profiles, ratings, and availability.

Booking Flow

Simple 3-step booking process with date selection, walker matching, and instant confirmation.

Live GPS Tracking

Real-time map showing walker's location, route taken, and estimated return time.

Activity Reports

Post-walk summary with photos, distance covered, and walker notes about your pet.

Methodology

Design Process

1

Research

Conducted user interviews with pet owners and dog walkers to identify pain points and opportunities.

2

Ideation

Created user personas, journey maps, and sketched initial concepts for key user flows.

3

Wireframing

Developed low-fidelity wireframes to establish information architecture and navigation patterns.

4

Visual Design

Created high-fidelity mockups with a cohesive design system and brand identity.

5

Prototyping

Built interactive prototypes in Figma to demonstrate user flows and micro-interactions.

6

Testing

Conducted usability tests and iterated based on feedback to refine the experience.
